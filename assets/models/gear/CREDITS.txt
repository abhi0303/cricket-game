The bat, pad and helmet in this folder are based on three models, adapted for the game by scripts/gear-model.html:

bat.glb (scaled to the game's 0.85 m bat, turned so its flat face is forward):
This work is based on "Cricket BAT 3D model SS" (https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/cricket-bat-3d-model-ss-757ec245077b4957820ad84ee746ecd6)
by knitro_vedant (https://sketchfab.com/knitro_vedant) licensed under CC-BY-4.0 (http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)

pad.glb (one pad of the pair, stood up, facing forward and centred on the leg; the game mirrors it for the other leg):
This work is based on "cricket leg ped" (https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/cricket-leg-ped-42f912de462d4522828ae04ca2363757)
by arogeek (https://sketchfab.com/arogeek) licensed under CC-BY-4.0 (http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)

helmet.glb (turned to face forward, its logos left out, split into shell, grille and screws; the game colours the shell):
This work is based on "Cricket Helmet_V1" (https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/cricket-helmet-v1-9b608ca09b8d46fd8579986d22c7ecb7)
by pradeepaanandhi5420 (https://sketchfab.com/pradeepaanandhi5420) licensed under CC-BY-4.0 (http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
